## Did
- Set up Fusion (education licence), created our team hub "Rc Car Hamza and Saad"
- Imported the OpenRC F1 2023 STEP file (Inverted Pursuits Lab remix of Daniel Norée's design), set units to mm
- Measured the stock spur gear: 40 teeth, 42 mm outside diameter, 12 mm thick → module = 42 ÷ (40 + 2) = 1.0
- Measured the motor mount: motor slot centres at Y 112.55–122.55 mm (Z 23.00), axle centre at Y 145.55 (Z 17.00)
  → motor-to-axle distance can be set from 23.77 to 33.54 mm
- Confirmed the axle bearings in the model are 12×18×4 (matches our 6701 bearings)

## Problems
- Our planned pinion (0.6 module, 14T) would NOT mesh with the stock module-1 spur
- Metal module-1 pinions only come with 5 mm bores; our 540 motor has a 1/8" (3.175 mm) shaft
- Stock printed pinion is a known weak point (shaft hole strips)

## Decision
- Switch to a metal 32-pitch 17T pinion (RRP0170, 1/8" bore) and redesign the spur gear as 32P 49T
- Check: centre distance = (17 + 49) ÷ 32 ÷ 2 × 25.4 = 26.19 mm → fits inside the 23.77–33.54 mm slot range
- Gear ratio 49 ÷ 17 = 2.88 vs stock 2.86 → about the same top speed
- New spur OD = 51 ÷ 32 × 25.4 = 40.48 mm → smaller than the stock 42 mm, so it fits
